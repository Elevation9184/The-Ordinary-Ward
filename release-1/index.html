How this roster model worksThe mechanics, in plain English
1 · Build once

A fixed two-year roster

The model creates 730 shifts. Day and night team sizes scale from 10-day / 5-night staffing in a 38-nurse ward.

2 · Allow real differences

Workloads are unequal

Each nurse receives a Beta-shaped relative workload normalized around the ward average, with separate day/night preferences.

3 · Replay the ward

Incidents move

Each history places incidents on distinct shifts. The chart shows one history; the lines summarize the highest overlap across the batch.

4 · Add benign exposure

The slider bends upward

The designated nurse starts at the ward average. Qualifications, helping and nights then multiply call-on weight nonlinearly.

For teaching, not clinical or legal inference. The simulation contains no wrongdoing mechanism and cannot evaluate a named ward, incident or person.
